What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize finds and compare source links, categories, sizing notes, listed prices and inspection photos. A well-kept list helps you revisit your choices; it does not guarantee availability, authenticity or product quality.

02 / Kakobuy shopping guide

From an interesting find
to an informed choice.

The most useful Kakobuy finds are the ones you can evaluate. Keep observations separate from assumptions, and leave room for questions before making a purchase.

Start your research ↗

  1. Choose the category first

    Use the Kakobuy spreadsheet categories as a browsing framework: footwear, clothing, headwear and accessories.

  2. Record the exact listing

    Save the source URL, variant, listed price, currency and date checked. Similar photos do not establish that two listings are identical.

  3. Review fit and visible details

    Use a Kakobuy QC checklist to inspect measurements, construction, color and any available inspection photos. Mark anything you cannot verify.

  4. Compare the complete cost

    Separate the item price from delivery, service fees and estimated taxes. Confirm current terms and the final payable amount before ordering.

03 / Your comparison framework

Give every column a job.

A compact sheet is easier to maintain. These fields help you compare products without turning your shortlist into a collection of unexplained links.

Suggested fields for your personal product spreadsheet
Field What to record Why it helps
Item & source Descriptive name, original product URL, seller and selected variant. Makes the exact listing easier to identify later.
Category Shoes, hoodies, T-shirts, jackets, pants, headwear or accessories. Keeps comparisons focused on similar products.
Sizing & material Size system, garment measurements, stated composition and missing details. Helps you identify fit questions before ordering.
Photos & observations Available inspection images, visible construction details and questions. Separates what is visible from what is merely claimed.
Cost breakdown Item price, currency, delivery, fees and clearly marked estimates. Prevents a low item price from hiding other costs.
Status & checked date Considering, needs information, shortlisted or removed; date last reviewed. Helps you spot entries that need another check.

This is a planning framework, not a live spreadsheet download. Product availability, prices, platform support and terms must be checked at the source.

Fit note

Compare measurements, not just letters.

A medium in one listing may differ from a medium in another. Compare the provided measurements with a garment you own, using the same measuring method where possible. Keep the seller’s size chart alongside your notes.

Photo note

Use images to ask better questions.

Look for clear views of seams, soles, closures and labels. Lighting can affect apparent color. Photos can reveal visible details, but cannot independently establish authenticity, seller authorization or long-term durability.

04 / Direct answers

Before you build your list.

Short answers to common questions about spreadsheet research, product comparisons and this guide.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize finds and compare details such as source links, categories, sizing notes, listed prices and inspection photos. Its usefulness depends on the accuracy and freshness of its entries.

Is this an official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent informational guide with links to CAFANS product categories. It does not represent Kakobuy or any featured brand.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a category directory and a comparison framework. You can create your own spreadsheet using the suggested fields and record the product links you choose.

What should I check before adding a product to my shortlist?

Check the exact variant, size chart, material description, source link, current listed price, available inspection photos and applicable return terms. Record any missing information instead of assuming it.

Can product photos prove authenticity?

No. Product and inspection photos can help assess visible details, but they do not independently prove authenticity, seller authorization or long-term durability.

How should I compare the total cost?

Record the item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties separately. Mark uncertain amounts as estimates and confirm the final amount before paying.
